Delete your data

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Last updated: October 19, 2026 · App: Wild Lens: Jungle Rescue (package com.charles.wildlens) · Developer: Hartmann Studios

Wild Lens has no account. Everything the game stores lives only on your phone, so you delete it yourself, right on the phone. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Delete your game data

Option 1: in the game. Tap NEW GAME on the title screen. This starts the story over from the first mission.
Option 2: uninstall. Uninstalling Wild Lens deletes everything the game stored: progress, FILM, your photo album and settings. You can also go to Android Settings → Apps → Wild Lens → Storage → Clear storage.

What is deleted

  • Game progress: story missions, stars, best scores, FILM, lenses and species photographed.
  • With Option 2, also your in-game photo album and your settings.

What is kept

  • Your purchases. Remove Ads and the Wild Lens VIP subscription are tied to your Google Play account, not to the game's storage. After reinstalling, tap Restore purchases in Settings to get them back. Uninstalling or deleting game data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions). To manage your Google Play purchase history, use your Google account.
  • Photos you shared. A photo you sent to another app with SHARE is now in that app, under its own rules.
  • Ad data held by Google. Google AdMob may hold data linked to your advertising ID under Google's policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, change your ad consent with Settings → Privacy options in the game, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

Need help?

If anything is unclear, open an issue on GitHub using the "Privacy or data question" template. Please don't post personal information in the issue.
