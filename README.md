# Wild Lens: Jungle Rescue

The website for **Wild Lens: Jungle Rescue**, a photoreal jungle adventure for Android by Hartmann Studios. You are Sam Reyes, a wildlife photographer in the Kavari Basin. With Pip, your tiny monkey best friend, photograph the wildlife, gather evidence against Vance Kessler's poacher ring and free the caged animals, across 5 chapters and 16 story missions. No account, no pay-to-win.
